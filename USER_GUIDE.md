# 🫧 LaundryHub — How to use it

A 60-second tour of everything you need to know.

---

## 1️⃣ Sign in

![Login](images/01-login.png)

- Open `https://laudryhub-v2.vercel.app`
- Tap **User**
- Type your **name** and **PIN** (your admin gave them to you)
- Tap **Sign In**

---

## 2️⃣ The home screen

![Home](images/02-home.png)

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| 🟢 **AVAILABLE** | Free — go ahead and start a wash |
| 🔴 **OFFLINE** | Machine is unplugged or no Wi-Fi — try later |
| 🔵 **IN USE** | Someone is washing right now |
| 🟡 **WASH DONE** | Cycle finished, relay cuts power in 5 min |

---

## 3️⃣ Start a wash

![Start](images/03-start.png)

1. Load your laundry on the machine, close the door.
2. Pick the cycle on the **machine itself**.
3. Press **Start** on the machine.
4. The app picks it up automatically within 10 seconds.

The big circle will spin and show:
- Cycle name (e.g. **Cotton 60°**)
- Phase (**Main wash → Rinse → Spin**)
- Live countdown (e.g. **42m**)
- Ends-at time (e.g. **Ends at 14:30**)

---

## 4️⃣ Pause / resume

![Pause](images/04-pause.png)

- Press the pause button on the machine.
- The app turns **red** and shows ⏸ **PAUSED** with the countdown frozen.
- Resume on the machine → app turns blue again, countdown ticks.

---

## 5️⃣ Wash done

![Done](images/05-done.png)

- The circle goes **amber** with ✓ **WASH DONE**.
- A 5-minute **Powering off in M:SS** countdown starts.
- Power cuts automatically at 0:00.
- Hit **🔌 Turn OFF now** if you want to cut early.

You'll get a phone notification *"Machine free!"* if you allowed notifications at sign-in.

---

## 6️⃣ Reserve a slot for later

![Schedule](images/06-schedule.png)

Scroll to **📅 Schedule a slot**:
- Pick **Date**, **Start**, **End**
- Tap **Reserve slot**
- Your reservation appears under **🗓️ Upcoming** for everyone to see

While your slot is active, no one else can start a wash.

---

## 7️⃣ Chat with housemates

![Chat](images/07-chat.png)

- Tap **Chat** in the top-right
- Pick a person (or **📢 Everyone**)
- Type a message → Enter
- Long-press your own messages → **✎ edit** or **🗑 delete**

---

## 8️⃣ Your wash history

![History](images/08-history.png)

Scroll to **✨ My washes**:
- 🏆 **Total** — lifetime wash count
- 📆 **Month** — washes this month
- ⏱ **Time** — total hours
- 💧 **Water** — total liters used
- ⚡ **Energy** — total kWh

Each row also shows water/energy per wash.

---

## ❓ Common questions

**"Why does it say machine offline when I'm sure it's plugged in?"**
The Wi-Fi or controller may have hiccuped. It auto-recovers within 5 min. If it persists past 10 min, tell admin.

**"I forgot my PIN."**
Ask your admin to reset it (they have a one-tap **PIN** button next to your name in admin → Users).

**"Can I install it like an app on my phone?"**
Yes — open the URL in Chrome → tap menu **⋮** → **Add to Home screen**. Same on Safari (share → Add to Home Screen).

**"It's stuck on a stale countdown."**
Pull-to-refresh or close-and-reopen the page. State catches up within 10 seconds.
